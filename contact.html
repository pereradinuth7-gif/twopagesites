Good to know

A few things
you might wonder.

Still have a question? Include it in your enquiry and we can discuss it together.

How long does a website take to build?

Timing depends on the agreed page count and features. We’ll discuss an estimated timeline after reviewing your project scope.

Do you provide hosting?

Domain and hosting are paid separately by the client and are not included unless explicitly agreed in the project scope.

Can I request changes after launch?

Additional pages, new features, and ongoing content changes are outside the Starter package. Each package includes a 30-day bug-fix warranty after launch; other updates can be discussed and scoped separately.

Do you work with businesses outside Colombo?

There’s no Colombo-only service area listed here. Share where you’re based in your enquiry so we can discuss your project.
